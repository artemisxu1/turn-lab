Website

Request a change

Describe what you’d like changed on the site in plain words. No need to know how the website is built.

Examples of requests that work well:

  • On the People page, change my title to Assistant Professor.
  • Add a news item dated 12 September 2026: our first paper was accepted.
  • Amin’s favourite animal is a snow leopard — it currently says PLACEHOLDER.
  • The third paragraph on Research is out of date. Replace it with: …

The more specific the better. If you want exact wording used, type it out and say “use these words exactly”.
