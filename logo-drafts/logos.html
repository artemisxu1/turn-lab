Variant G — Phenotype ⇄ signature clock

Each hour on the face carries a pair: a molecular signature inside the cytoplasm, tied across the membrane to the phenotype it produces — round, migrating, dividing, spread. A sweep of light selects one hour and that tie goes solid — with a receptor firing where it crosses the membrane — so the mark says the thing the lab says: the molecular state you are in, and what the cell becomes, depend on when you look.
