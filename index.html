Standing at the Crossroads.

We study how cells decide their fate — mapping the transient signaling events in time and space that govern cell cycle exit, transition into G0, and commitment to tissue-specific trajectories.

This background represents how we think of dynamic signaling in protein networks. Try to break a bond and see what happens!
