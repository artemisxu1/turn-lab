Mission

Our mission

The overarching goal of the Turn lab to understand the fundamental mechanisms driving signaling in time and space and how these are disrupted in the context of pathology. We have turned our focus to the following goals.

  1. 01

    Mechanism

    To address the question of what exactly drives commitment to G0 and fate specific programs and how these are disrupted in pathology. Our goal is to do so with rigor and reproducibility, making lasting contributions to our burgeoning field.

  2. 02

    People

    To train the next generation of scientists from all walks of life. We will encourage a diverse, rich, inclusive, and open research community where all are welcome at the table.

  3. 03

    Impact

    The generation of scientific discoveries that may pave the way for new medical treatments that will enable people to increase both healthspan and lifespan.
